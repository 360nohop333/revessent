Who we are

Revessent ("we") operates revenue-recovery software for subscription businesses. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, and the choices available. It is written to be compatible with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and, where it applies to EU/UK residents, the GDPR.

Data we process

  • Account data — your email, authentication identifiers and workspace role.
  • Billing connection data — the API keys and webhook secret you provide for your billing platform (secrets are stored encrypted where technically supported; keys are never displayed again after entry).
  • Customer data you send us — your customers' names, emails, phone numbers, payment amounts, payment method references and failure reasons, received via your billing platform's webhook.
  • Operational data — recovery cases, retry attempts, messages drafted and sent, and delivery events.

Why we process it

Solely to operate the service: detecting failed payments, scheduling and attempting recoveries, drafting and sending recovery messages on your instruction, attributing recovered revenue, and keeping the service secure. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Sub-processors

  • Vercel — application hosting (EU/US regions).
  • Neon — PostgreSQL database storage.
  • Supabase — authentication.
  • Google (Gemini) — drafting recovery message text. Prompts include your customer's first name, amount and failure reason; no full card or bank details are ever included.
  • Resend — email delivery.
  • Razorpay — payment retries and payment links, directly between you and Razorpay.

Retention

Account and operational data are kept while your workspace is active. Raw webhook payloads may contain customer contact details; they are retained for deduplication and replay for 30 days and then deleted automatically by the nightly scheduler (configurable per deployment). Deleting your workspace removes its data immediately.

Your rights

Workspace owners can export (CSV) or delete their data by contacting hello@revessent.com. If you are a consumer whose data was shared by a business using Revessent, you may contact us or that business to exercise access, correction, erasure or objection rights.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted (TLS). Secrets are encrypted at rest. Access is role-restricted. We never store raw card numbers — Revessent works only with billing-platform tokens.
