Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are & Overview

Revessent ("we", "us", or "our") operates payment recovery and subscription intelligence software. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal data we collect, how it is processed and protected, our third-party sub-processors, and your rights concerning that data.

2. Data We Collect

We process information belonging to two primary groups: direct workspace users (our customers) and the end-customers of those businesses.

  • Account Data: When you register for Revessent, we collect your email address, workspace name, user identifier, and workspace role (owner, admin, member).
  • Billing Connection Credentials: When you integrate your payment processor (Razorpay), we securely store your API Key ID and Key Secret. Secret keys are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption and are never exposed in responses after entry.
  • Your End-Customers' Payment Data: As part of processing recovery workflows via your connected Razorpay account, Revessent receives and processes your customers' names, email addresses, telephone numbers (for recovery communications and SMS updates), subscription IDs, payment transaction amounts, currency codes, and payment failure/decline reasons.
  • Operational & Recovery Records: We generate and store records of payment failure cases, retry attempts, recovery email/SMS drafts and delivery timestamps, unsubscribe suppression lists, and administrative audit trails.

3. How We Use Data

We process personal data strictly to deliver and maintain the Revessent platform, including:

  • Detecting payment failure events in real time and scheduling smart retries.
  • Drafting tailored recovery emails and SMS notifications addressed to affected subscribers.
  • Executing authorized recovery notifications upon human or automated approval.
  • Measuring and reporting recovered subscription revenue in your workspace ledger.
  • Enforcing recipient opt-outs and suppression preferences.

We do not sell personal data, use it for cross-site behavioral profiling, or market third-party products to your end-customers.

4. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

Data is stored in isolated PostgreSQL instances hosted on Neon. All database connections and API communications require Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+). Sensitive credentials (such as Razorpay API secrets and webhook secrets) are encrypted before database insertion using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with unique initialization vectors.

Revessent does not store credit card numbers, CVVs, card PINs, or raw banking authentication credentials. All payment processing transactions are executed directly by Razorpay.

5. Authorized Sub-Processors

To provide our services, we partner with the following infrastructure and service sub-processors:

  • Supabase — User identity management, session tokens, and authentication services.
  • Neon — Managed Serverless PostgreSQL database storage.
  • Razorpay — Payment gateway integration, payment retry orchestration, and checkout processing.
  • Google (Gemini API) — AI-assisted drafting of recovery email and SMS copy. Message prompts contain minimal contextual data (customer first name, transaction amount, decline reason); full financial credentials or banking details are never sent.
  • Resend — Transactional email delivery and bounce/complaint webhook processing.
  • Vercel — Application compute hosting and serverless edge delivery.

6. Data Retention

Active workspace data, customer records, and recovery ledgers are retained for the duration of your active workspace subscription. Raw webhook payload records ingested from payment gateways are retained for 30 days for deduplication and transaction replay purposes, after which they are permanently purged by automated daily sweeps.

7. Data Subject Rights & Deletion

Workspace owners can export customer lists and case histories as CSV files directly from the dashboard. Workspace owners may delete their entire organization from Workspace Settings, which triggers a complete cascading purge of associated cases, recovery attempts, notes, keys, and member records.

End-customers who receive recovery messages can opt out instantly via the one-click unsubscribe link included in all communications. For access, rectification, or erasure inquiries, users and end-customers may contact legal@revessent.com.

8. Cookies & Tracking

Revessent uses essential first-party cookies and browser local storage solely to preserve authentication tokens and theme preferences. There are currently no third-party advertising or marketing analytics trackers loaded on the marketing website or application dashboard.

9. DPDP Act (India) & GDPR Acknowledgment

Revessent is architected with privacy and data minimization principles in mind. As a product in active pilot and beta development, formal compliance programs and contractual frameworks under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for European data subjects are actively ongoing. We make transparent and honest representations regarding our ongoing data governance measures.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data handling practices, please write to us at legal@revessent.com.
